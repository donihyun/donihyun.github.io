Solving exp(exp(z)) = 1 on the Complex Plane

Introduction

In this note, we solve the equation

eez=1,

for all complex numbers z∈C .

A useful idea is to first solve a simpler outer equation, and then pull it back through the inner exponential.

Step 1: Solve ew=1

For complex w ,

ew=1⟺w=2πik,  k∈Z.

Now let w=ez . Then

ez=2πik,k∈Z.

Since ez≠0 , the case k=0 is impossible. So we only consider k∈Z∖{0} .

Step 2: Solve ez=2πik

Write z=x+iy with x,y∈R . Then

ez=ex+iy=ex(cos⁡y+isin⁡y).

Matching real and imaginary parts with 2πik :

excos⁡y=0,exsin⁡y=2πk.

Because ex>0 , the first equation gives cos⁡y=0 , so

y=π2+nπ,n∈Z.

Hence sin⁡y=±1 , and we split by the sign of k :

  • If k>0 , then sin⁡y=1 , so

    y=π2+2πm,m∈Z,

    and

    x=ln⁡(2πk).

  • If k<0 , then sin⁡y=−1 , so

    y=3π2+2πm,m∈Z,

    and

    x=ln⁡(−2πk).

Final Solution Set

Therefore,

{z=ln⁡(2πk)+i(π2+2πm),k>0,  k,m∈Z,z=ln⁡(−2πk)+i(3π2+2πm),k<0,  k,m∈Z.

Equivalently, with k∈Z∖{0} :

z=ln⁡(2π|k|)+i(arg⁡(ik)+2πm),m∈Z,

where arg⁡(ik)=π2 when k>0 , and arg⁡(ik)=3π2 when k<0 (mod 2π ).
